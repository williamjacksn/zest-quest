87 robots.
A world of hope.

The Fuzzy Plague is consuming your home. Of the 87 operational Vessy robots in Sector 04, you’re number 457. Your creators gave you a spaceship, a citric scanner, and a mission: find somewhere they can live.

01Run, jump, then jump againSpace / W / ↑ jumps. Press again in midair for a double jump. Hold jump to thrust on any planet; land to refill fuel. Shift / X dashes. S / ↓ drops through ledges.
02Learn each world’s tricksRide lifts, bounce on jelly, swim through currents, and time the lasers. Your blaster fires automatically; E pulses nearby enemies away.
03Bring it homeTouch all five glowing seed cores and reach the ship at the far end. Beacons save your place after a fall. Between worlds, explore the ship and press E at an object to choose an upgrade. Follow the yellow arrows to the Airlock when you’re ready.
